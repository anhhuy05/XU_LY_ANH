1. MUC DICH VA PHAM VI
----------------------
Du an phat hien vach lan tren anh duong pho trong dieu kien chieu sang khong dong deu.
Hai bo phat hien duoc doi chieu:
  - Baseline: Gaussian blur -> Canny voi nguong co dinh -> Hough cua OpenCV.
  - P (proposed): chuan hoa chieu sang -> Canny tu cai dat -> Hough gioi han huong theo gradient.

Ket qua cua P khong duoc mac dinh xem la tot hon baseline. Can bao cao F1 theo tung tang,
ghi ro so anh, dung vai tro dev/eval va neu han che ve du lieu.

2. LUONG XU LY TONG THE
-----------------------
Du lieu anh + nhan .lines.txt
    -> tools.make_stratification (thong ke bo du lieu, neu can)
    -> utils.load_items / _read (doc anh, doc toa do nhan, resize)
    -> run_baseline va run_proposed (du doan vach lan)
    -> utils.match_lanes (so khop du doan voi nhan)
    -> utils.evaluate / evaluate_by_tier (tong hop P, R, F1, sai lech va thoi gian)
    -> experiments E1-E4 (bang CSV, hinh PNG, anh minh hoa).

Duong chay tong hop trong run_all.py:
    1) tools.make_stratification
    2) experiments.e2_params
    3) experiments.e1_challenge
    4) experiments.e3_ablation
    5) experiments.e4_breaking_point
E2 chay truoc vi no luu results/best_params.json de cac buoc sau nap lai.
compare_images.py va export_all.py la cac cong cu chay rieng, khong nam trong run_all.py.

- Dev duoc dung de chon tham so; eval duoc dung de bao cao ket qua. Tuy nhien, bo eval

4. HAI PIPELINE PHAT HIEN
-------------------------
4.1 Baseline (baseline.py)
  Buoc 1: tao ROI hinh thang chung tu utils.roi_mask.
  Buoc 2: lam mo Gaussian 5x5 de giam nhieu.
  Buoc 3: cv2.Canny voi b_lo va b_hi trong Params.
  Buoc 4: loai canh ngoai ROI.
  Buoc 5: cv2.HoughLines tim duong trong hai khoang goc; bo qua duong gan ngang theo
          MIN_HORIZ_DEG; nguong vote dung b_thresh.
  Buoc 6: utils.postprocess_lines loc duong gan ngang, loai duong trung gan nhau va
          giu toi da MAX_LANES duong.
  Dau ra: danh sach duong (rho, theta, score); debug=True tra them anh canh.

4.2 Phuong phap de xuat P (proposed.py)
  Buoc 1 - ROI rieng: tao ROI P bang p_roi_top, p_roi_x_left, p_roi_x_right.
          ROI nay chi gioi han phat hien; ROI cham diem van dung ROI chung.
  Buoc 2 - loc va chuan hoa: Gaussian sigma; neu use_norm=True thi chia anh loc cho
          local mean + 1. Local mean tinh nhanh bang integral image voi cua so radius.
  Buoc 3 - gradient: tinh gx, gy bang Sobel tu cai dat; magnitude = sqrt(gx^2 + gy^2),
          huong gradient tinh bang atan2.
  Buoc 4 - tao canh: non-maximum suppression luong tu huong thanh 4 nhom; hysteresis
          gom cac diem canh yeu lien thong voi diem vuot nguong manh.
          Nguong chon theo anh da chuan hoa hoac raw, sau do nhan thr_scale.
  Buoc 5 - Hough theo gradient: moi diem canh bo phieu vao cac goc gan huong gradient
          trong khoang delta; use_vote=False thi bo phieu tren moi goc.
          Bo dem Hough su dung buoc rho/theta mac dinh 2 px/1 do.
  Buoc 6 - tim dinh: lam min bo dem neu smooth > 0, non-maximum suppression tren
          accumulator, loc dinh theo nguong vote va lay cac dinh manh nhat.
  Buoc 7: postprocess chung loai duong khong hop le/trung nhau va gioi han so duong.
  Debug mode tra edges, norm, mag va accumulator A de tao hinh trung gian.

4.3 Tham so dang luu trong results/best_params.json
  Baseline: b_lo=30, b_hi=90, b_thresh=25.
  P: use_norm=true, use_vote=true, sigma=1.5, radius=15, thr_scale=0.45,
     delta=6.0, smooth=0.0, peak_nms=3.
  ROI P: top=0.55, x_left=0.35, x_right=0.60.
  Day la tham so dang co trong file hien tai; neu E2 chay lai, JSON co the thay doi.

5. CACH TINH DIEM
-----------------
- utils.match_lanes ve moi vach thanh mask co be rong gan LANE_W * SCALE.
- Phan tren ROI_TOP bi cat bo truoc khi tinh giao/hop.
- Tinh IoU giua tung duong du doan va tung vach ground truth.
- Dung Hungarian assignment de ghep cap toi uu; cap chi tinh TP neu IoU > IOU_THRESH
  (mac dinh 0.5). Du doan khong ghep la FP; vach nhan khong ghep la FN.
- Precision = TP/(TP+FP), Recall = TP/(TP+FN), F1 = 2TP/(2TP+FP+FN).
- F1 tong hop la micro-F1 tren tong TP/FP/FN, khong phai trung binh F1 tung anh.
- Sai lech ngang tinh tai hang y=0.9H tren cac vach ghep dung; thoi gian xu ly la ms/anh.
- utils.evaluate_by_tier tach ket qua theo ten tier cua Item.

6. CHI TIET TUNG FILE PYTHON
----------------------------
6.1 File o thu muc goc

baseline.py
  - canny_fixed: Gaussian blur + Canny co dinh + mask ROI.
  - hough_standard: goi HoughLines cua OpenCV cho hai khoang theta; tra rho, theta, score.
  - run_baseline: noi pipeline baseline; debug=True tra them edges.

config.py
  - ROOT, DATA_ROOT, DATA, RESULTS: xac dinh thu muc du an va data/results.
  - resolve_data_path: chuan hoa duong dan va tu choi duong dan nam ngoai data/.
  - DATA co the doi bang bien moi truong LANE_DATA nhung van phai nam trong data/.
  - _tiers/TIERS: phat hien cac thu muc tier trong eval.
  - Params: tap hop tham so cho baseline va P.
  - Cac hang so ROI, scale anh, rong vach nhan, nguong IoU va so vach toi da.

proposed.py
  - integral_image/local_mean: tinh anh tich phan va trung binh cuc bo.
  - illumination_norm: loc Gaussian, tuy chon chuan hoa theo nen sang cuc bo.
  - sobel: tinh gradient gx/gy.
  - non_max_suppression: giu cuc dai gradient doc theo huong.
  - hysteresis/canny_adaptive: tao canh bang nguong yeu/manh va lien thong.
  - hough_vote: cong don diem vao accumulator, co the gioi han theo gradient.
  - find_peaks/hough_gradient: loc va chon cac duong Hough manh.
  - run_proposed: noi cac buoc P va tuy chon tra thong tin debug.

utils.py
  - Item: goi thong tin tier, path, anh grayscale va ground truth.
  - roi_mask/line_x/postprocess_lines: tao ROI, chuyen duong sang toa do x va loc duong.
  - _images/_read/load_items: tim anh co nhan, doc/resize anh va nap split.
  - match_lanes: tao mask, tinh IoU, ghep Hungarian va tra TP/FP/FN.
  - evaluate/evaluate_by_tier: tong hop chi so va tach theo tier.
  - illum_stats: do do lech chuan, illum_ratio va mean brightness trong ROI.
  - write_table/figures_dir: tao thu muc va ghi CSV/hinh ket qua.
  - overlay: ve du doan do; co tuy chon ve ground truth va anh canh.
  - load_params/save_params: nap/ghi results/best_params.json.
  - video_id: rut ten video goc tu ten anh frame.

run_all.py
  - Nhan tuy chon --max va chuyen tham so nay cho cac thi nghiem.
  - Chay tung module bang subprocess voi check=True; loi o mot buoc se dung chuoi chay.
  - Khong chay compare_images.py hoac export_all.py.

6.2 Thu muc experiments

experiments\__init__.py
  - Danh dau thu muc la package; khong chua pipeline xu ly rieng.

experiments\e1_challenge.py
  - Nap tham so va anh eval; chay baseline tren toan bo anh.
  - Nhom ket qua theo tier; tinh precision, recall, F1, sai lech va thong ke chieu sang.
  - Ghi results/tables/e1_baseline.csv.
  - Tim 3 anh co F1 thap nhat moi tier va ghi vao results/failure_cases/.

experiments\e2_params.py
  - Nap dev va gan lai tier theo thu muc cha normal/hlight/shadow.
  - Tinh illum_ratio; lay nguong phan vi 75% va tao nhom anh contrast cao.
  - Tinh F1 shadow, F1 tung video tren nhom contrast va F1 cac tier con lai.
  - Diem chon = 0.50*F1_shadow + 0.25*contrast_video_F1
                 + 0.25*mean_F1_cac_tier_khac.
  - Tim baseline tham khao; chi chap nhan candidate P neu F1 shadow >= baseline shadow dev.
  - Quet lan luot thr_scale, radius, delta, smooth, peak_nms va hinh hoc ROI P;
    tinh them mot luot tinh chinh thr_scale.
  - Luu tham so P, CSV sweep, do thi tham so va hinh cac buoc trung gian.
  - Luu y: sweep la coordinate search, khong phai tim kiem toan cuc tren moi to hop tham so.

experiments\e3_ablation.py
  - Nap tham so duoc E2 chon; nap eval va dev.
  - Tren dev, chon thr_scale cho bien the tat chuan hoa trong tap {0.5, 1, 2, 4}.
  - Tren eval, so sanh P day du, P use_norm=False, P use_vote=False va baseline.
  - Ghi F1/precision/recall/sai lech/thoi gian vao e3_ablation.csv.
  - Ve e3_ablation.png de so sanh F1 theo tier.

experiments\e4_breaking_point.py
  - Chi nap eval/normal lam can cu tao bong tong hop.
  - Do tuong phan median giua vach va vung xung quanh; in du doan d* truoc vong thi nghiem.
  - Tao mask bong logistic voi hai do mem bien; giam cuong do anh theo cac depth co dinh
    va them nhieu Gaussian theo sigma noise.
  - Chay baseline/P tren tung depth; tim diem dau tien F1 con duoi 50% F1 tai depth 0.
  - Ghi results/tables/e4_break.csv va results/figures/e4_break.png.
  - Day la thi nghiem bong nhan tao, khong phai tap shadow tu nhien.

experiments\compare_images.py
  - Mac dinh danh gia tung anh eval, xep theo chenh lech F1 P - baseline.
  - Xuat toi da k anh P thang moi tier; --all se chon anh baseline kem nhat neu khong co P thang.
  - --image cho phep chi dinh mot anh trong data/; duong dan ngoai data/ bi tu choi.
  - Anh ghep gom anh goc, ket qua hai detector, anh chuan hoa va hai ban do canh.

experiments\export_all.py
  - Chay baseline va P tren eval; --dev de them dev, --max de gioi han so anh moi tier.
  - Voi moi anh tao panel goc, overlay du doan, anh chuan hoa va edges.
  - Ghi anh vao results/all_images/<tier>/ va F1 tung anh vao results/tables/per_image.csv.

  - F1 P bien cung giam tu 0.263 xuong 0.152; bien mem tai 0.95 dat 0.212.
  - Trong cac depth da thu, khong dong nao cua P hoac baseline giam duoi mot nua F1 goc;
